Frequently Asked Questions

What's included in the Free plan?

The Free plan gives you 1 lifetime AI interview session, resume analysis (speech module only), top 3 AI job matches, and placement readiness score. Upgrade to Premium for unlimited access.

How does the Premium plan work?

Premium unlocks all features: unlimited interviews, full resume analysis (attire + body language), AI Career Mentor, Mock Placement Drive, Panel Interview, AI Recruiter Dashboard, and more.

Is there an Institution plan?

Yes! Institutions get everything in Premium plus batch analytics, weak-area reports, PDF/CSV exports, and configurable seat limits. Contact sales for custom pricing.
